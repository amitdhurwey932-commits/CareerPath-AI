Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2024 · CareerPath AI College Project

What Data We Collect
  • Registration data: Username, email address, hashed password, and education level.
  • Profile information: Degree, interests, skills, and career goals — provided voluntarily by you.
  • Usage data: Career recommendations, skill assessments, roadmap progress, saved projects, and interview practice sessions.
  • We do not collect payment information, national ID, or sensitive personal details.
Why We Collect It
  • To personalise your career recommendations and learning roadmaps.
  • To save your skill assessments and roadmap progress across sessions.
  • To provide you with a consistent, personalised experience on the platform.
How We Store It
  • Passwords are hashed using Werkzeug's secure password hashing (PBKDF2). Plain-text passwords are never stored.
  • All data is stored in a local SQLite database on the server running this application.
  • This is a college project running locally or on a controlled server. Your data is not shared with third parties.
AI API Usage

If an AI API key is configured, some of your profile information (interests, skills, career goals) may be sent to a third-party AI provider to enhance recommendations or feedback. No personally identifying information (name, email) is sent to the AI provider. API keys are stored as environment variables and never exposed in frontend code.

Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting the project administrator. This is a college project. Data is used solely for demonstrating the platform's functionality.
