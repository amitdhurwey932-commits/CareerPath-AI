About CareerPath AI

An AI-powered career guidance platform for students and graduates

Project Objective

CareerPath AI was built as a college project to demonstrate how artificial intelligence and modern web technology can support career guidance and skill development for students, fresh graduates, and job seekers.

The platform helps users discover suitable career options, identify skill gaps, generate personalised learning roadmaps, find practical project ideas, prepare resumes, and practise for interviews.

All career recommendations are for guidance purposes only. They do not guarantee employment or accurately predict career success.

SDG 8 — Decent Work & Economic Growth

This project directly supports United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 8, which promotes sustained, inclusive, and sustainable economic growth, full and productive employment, and decent work for all.
